About TopMark Tutorial Center

TopMark Tutorial Center is a tutorial school in Kishi, Oyo State, set up to help students understand their subjects deeply, build confidence and perform well in their examinations.

Our tutors break every topic into clear, simple steps, give students well-prepared lesson notes, and keep a close eye on each student's progress through regular tests and practicals.

Our mission

To provide quality teaching and individual attention in a friendly environment, so every student can reach their goals.

Admissions: how to enrol

Registering takes a few minutes. Classes begin on Thursday, 5th November 2026.

Get the registration form

Collect the Student Registration Form at the center or ask for it by phone or WhatsApp.

Fill it in and sign

Complete the student details, add a parent or guardian phone number, and have the student and parent/guardian sign.

Attach a passport photograph

Paste one recent passport photograph in the space on the form.

Pay the registration fee

Pay ₦500 at the center to complete your registration and receive your Admission Number.

Frequently asked questions

When do classes start?

Classes start on Thursday, 5th November 2026.

Where and when are classes held?
